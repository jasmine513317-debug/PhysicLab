# Known limitations

- The workbenches support 2D and 3D mechanics and specialized educational science models. Linear medium damping is available in 2D. Fluids, deformable solids, fracture, relativistic effects and full-wave electromagnetics are outside these models. A 2D support-force target requires fixed support, a common contact normal and a stable normal velocity. For a dynamic support or contacts in different directions, apply an external force and inspect the reactions computed by the solver.
- Finite steps, the contact solver, and the engine's movement limits affect very fast bodies or extreme mass and size ratios. Spring stiffness is constrained by the frequency limit. An off-center spring attachment changes the effective oscillation dynamics.
- The projectile reference assumes constant gravity and air resistance of zero. Comparisons against a flat floor assume the projectile reaches its top without first hitting another object.
- Energy is tracked per body, including an allocated share of spring potential energy. External work, friction, damping, and collisions can change it.
- Data samples are retained for roughly four minutes per body at the full sample rate. Comparisons after the recording window rolls over describe the retained portion. The scene editor caps a scene at 100 bodies and 200 joints. Scenes with at least 40 bodies render the live canvas at up to 30 frames per second while retaining 60 Hz fixed physics steps and 15 Hz recording.
- The workspace keeps one saved 2D replay per experiment and one recently completed run in memory. Replay preserves poses, velocities, accelerations and the intervention sequence, within the retained recording span. The spatial workspace has independent checkpoints and a combined file that also records the planar event sequence, camera and analysis options. Export JSON and CSV to keep copies outside browser storage.
- IndexedDB is local to the current origin and browser profile. A private browsing policy, storage quota, or cleared site data can affect the library. Export scene JSON to keep a portable copy.
- Automated browser coverage uses Edge, Playwright Firefox, and Playwright WebKit on Windows. Mobile coverage uses viewport and touch emulation. Native Safari and physical phone and tablet testing require the corresponding devices.
- A 3D extrusion or revolved dynamic mesh uses a convex hull collider. A fixed track or surface uses a triangulated mesh. A lossy projection or section creates an independent 2D copy. A geometric map into a different contact solver cannot guarantee identical collision response.
- The first 3D view downloads the pinned Rapier WASM payload. Field visualization is sampled, prediction covers two simulated seconds, and the live 3D recording has a bounded history. Device-specific frame rate and long-run memory still require profiling on target hardware.
